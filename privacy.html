QuoteCraft Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026
App: QuoteCraft — Quotation & Invoice Maker (com.vivek.quotecraft) and its web app
Publisher: QuoteCraft
Contact: noreplyautoengine@gmail.com

Short version. QuoteCraft is a local-first business document app. The publisher does not operate a server that receives your quotations, customers or files. Workspace data stays on your device unless you choose to sync it through one file in your own Google Drive.

Summary

  • Workspace data is stored on your device or in your browser.
  • Local accounts and local use work without Google.
  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive sync are optional.
  • If you connect Google Drive, QuoteCraft reads and writes only one shared JSON sync file that it creates for this app and Google account, or that you explicitly select with the Google Picker, using the drive.file scope. The same file can be opened by QuoteCraft on Android and on the web with the same Google account.
  • The publisher does not receive, sell or share your workspace data.
  • QuoteCraft contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Information stored on your device or browser

Android may store:

  • Account name, email address, a salted and hashed local password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256), the Google account identifier when you link Google, and session state. These are kept in Android Keystore-backed secure storage.
  • An optional app-lock PIN hash and biometric setting. Biometric matching is performed by Android; QuoteCraft never receives biometric templates.
  • A random sync device identifier and sync flags.
  • A small local diagnostics log (the text of unexpected errors, up to the last 100 entries). It never leaves the device and you can clear it in Help & FAQ → Diagnostics.

The web app may store in browser storage:

  • The signed-in Google account's email and display name.
  • The selected sync file ID and name, last successful sync time, a random device identifier and whether sync is paused.
  • Google access tokens are kept in memory only and are not written to browser storage.

Both clients store business profiles, logos, signatures, stamps, GSTIN/PAN, bank/UPI details, customer and product records, quotations, invoices, payments, notes, terms, custom fields and settings.

Android workspace records are stored in an app-private SQLite database, and Android automatic backup is disabled. The web workspace is stored in the browser's IndexedDB. Local passwords and PINs are not recoverable by the publisher.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive

If you choose Continue with Google or Connect Google Drive on Android, or sign in with Google in the web app, Google provides QuoteCraft with your Google account name, email address and account identifier so it can link the workspace to that account. QuoteCraft requests the Google OAuth scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

QuoteCraft also requests openid, email and profile on web so it can show the signed-in account. The drive.file scope lets QuoteCraft create files and access only files that it created or that you explicitly select with the Google Picker. It does not grant access to the rest of your Drive.

On Android, QuoteCraft creates the shared JSON file named quotecraft-sync-v2.json on first use. On another phone signed into the same Google account, it finds that app-created file automatically and restores it; no Picker is needed. If two phones each created a sync file, Android merges every copy into one file and renames the older copies (it does not delete them). On the web, you select the same file once with the Google Picker because the web client is a separate OAuth client; the browser remembers that file for the signed-in Google account, so later visits restore it without the Picker. The file is visible in your My Drive and contains your workspace data: business profiles, customers, products, quotations, invoices, payments, line groups and settings. Keep it private and do not share it. The Picker or automatic app-only discovery grants QuoteCraft access to that file; it does not grant access to the rest of your Drive.

Google Drive sync is on by default after a sync file is selected. When online, QuoteCraft merges local changes into the file shortly after they are saved, and merges changes from other devices when the app or browser is opened or refreshed, returns to the foreground, or when you tap Sync now. On Android a background worker may also check the same file about every 15 minutes while the phone is online and a Google account is linked; it performs only this sync, and pausing or deleting the Drive copy stops it. Records are merged by stable IDs. If two devices use the same quotation number for distinct documents, both are preserved; drafts may be renumbered and issued documents are flagged for number review. If the same record is edited on two devices at once, the most recently updated version wins.

The data is transferred over HTTPS directly between QuoteCraft and Google's Drive API. It is stored in your Google account under Google's terms and privacy policy. The publisher does not operate a server in this path and does not receive a copy of the file. Google may process account and usage information under its own policies; see Google's Privacy Policy.

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use. QuoteCraft's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can stop using Google at any time. Delete Drive copy in Account & sync (Android) or Google Drive sync (web) deletes the shared sync file and pauses sync. You can also revoke QuoteCraft's access at Google Account permissions. Revoking access does not by itself delete the file already stored in Drive; use the in-app delete control or Google's account tools.

Information the publisher does not collect

The publisher does not receive:

  • your quotations, invoices, customer or product records;
  • your passwords, PINs or biometric data;
  • your location, contacts, SMS, call logs, photos or files outside the chosen QuoteCraft sync file;
  • advertising identifiers or analytics/telemetry from QuoteCraft.

QuoteCraft contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK. Unexpected errors are written to a local diagnostics log that you can read and clear in Help & FAQ; it is never uploaded. QuoteCraft does not process payments or connect to a bank.

Sharing, printing and exports

PDFs, CSV files and backups leave the app only when you choose to share, print or export them. Android or the browser then passes the file to the app or service you select (for example Gmail, WhatsApp or a printer). That recipient handles the file under its own privacy policy. QuoteCraft does not upload those files to the publisher. Local backup files you create remain wherever you save them until you delete them.

Permissions

  • INTERNET — used only for optional Google Sign-In, Google Picker and Google Drive sync. Local accounts and local work continue to function offline.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used to decide when pending sync work can run. It does not access network traffic or personal data.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT — used only if you enable biometric app unlock. Android performs the matching.
  • File picker access — used only when you choose a logo, signature, stamp, backup file, sync file or export destination.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or broad storage permissions.

Security

Android workspace data is kept in app-private storage. Account passwords are salted and hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (150,000 iterations). Google session tokens are held by Google Sign-In; the web access token is held in memory only. The QuoteCraft database does not store Google access tokens. Google Drive traffic uses HTTPS. Optional local backup files can be encrypted with AES-GCM using a password-derived key.

The web access token expires after a period and must be reacquired by signing in again; the web app does not use a backend or refresh token. No security measure is perfect. Use a device screen lock, keep your passwords safe, and maintain backups. The publisher cannot recover a forgotten local password, PIN or encrypted-backup password.

Retention, deletion and your choices

  • You can edit or delete customers, products, draft documents and businesses inside the app. Deleting a business removes its associated records after confirmation.
  • Account & sync → Delete Drive copy deletes the shared QuoteCraft sync file from Google Drive and pauses further uploads.
  • Account & sync → Erase all local data & accounts permanently removes the local workspace, all local accounts, the app PIN and local sync state on the phone. It does not delete a Drive copy; use Delete Drive copy first for that.
  • In the web app, Erase all data clears the browser workspace and disconnects Google sync; the Drive file is left untouched unless you choose Delete Drive copy first.
  • Signing out or disconnecting Google leaves the local workspace in place. Uninstalling the app removes the Android app-private database and secure-storage accounts, subject to Android behavior. Clearing browser storage removes the web workspace.
  • Local backup files and files you shared remain wherever you saved or sent them.
  • To remove Google authorization, use the in-app delete control and/or Google Account permissions.

Because the publisher does not operate an account server, there is no publisher-side account or workspace data to delete. For privacy questions, contact noreplyautoengine@gmail.com.

Children

QuoteCraft is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed to children. The publisher does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
